## Diagnosis checkpoint

The initial enclosing ellipse failed before scene export at **17.531666 mm**
maximum expansion. The actual-source `test_panel_cage.py --budget-only` command
reproduced failure in about two seconds of test time. Reducing to the uppermost
section retained the same failure.

Three hypotheses were tested: ellipse orientation/center, longitudinal
interpolation, and unsuitable ellipse shape for source folds. Removing
interpolation still gave 17.531666 mm. PCA rotation gave 15.609674 mm, still above
15 mm. Therefore interpolation was not the root cause; orientation alone was
insufficient. The low-dimensional periodic spline retained the same budget and
passed at 12.240843 mm. The original CLI and saved-scene tests were rerun. Debug
probes were removed; no quality limits were relaxed.
